# @CorporatePranks Instagram Caption Style Guide

> Derived from analysis of 100 posts (scraped March 14, 2026)
> Account: 385 posts | 53.7K followers | 119 following

---

## Brand Voice Summary

**CorporatePranks writes like a cynical Roman historian commenting on modern corporate and geopolitical absurdity.** The tone is intellectual but accessible — think a philosophy professor who moonlights as a stand-up comedian observing the parallels between ancient empires and modern cubicle life.

**Core identity:** "Corporate tyranny posted daily. Ancient Rome parallels."

---

## Caption Architecture

### Structure (5 paragraphs is the sweet spot)

| Paragraphs | Frequency | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| 5 | **50%** of posts | The dominant format |
| 6 | 16% | For deeper topics |
| 1 | 11% | Quick reactive takes |
| 7+ | 13% | Long-form essays |

**Standard template:**
1. **Title** (standalone line, bold/declarative)
2. **Blank line**
3. **Opening paragraph** — set the scene, reference the video/image content
4. **Middle paragraph(s)** — the Roman parallel or historical connection
5. **"The prank" paragraph** — the signature closing insight
6. **Blank line**
7. **Hashtag block** (2-5 hashtags)

### Length
- **Average:** 1,576 characters (~250 words)
- **Median:** 1,750 characters
- **Range:** 6 to 2,190 characters
- **Target:** Aim for 1,400–2,000 characters (the engagement sweet spot)

## The "Prank" Motif (Signature Move)

**54% of captions include the phrase "The prank is..."** — this is the account's rhetorical signature. It reframes the topic as a cosmic joke or systemic deception.

### How it works
The "prank" line typically appears in the **final paragraph** as the thesis statement — the big insight that ties the modern observation to historical pattern.

### Real examples:
> "The prank is the rat race — a trap so well-designed that most of us don't even realize we're caught in it until the years have already blurred together."

> "The prank is the job itself: a mechanism of distraction that forces you to channel your creative energy into someone else's monument."

> "The prank is thinking you can trade your soul for 'safety' and still think you're American."

> "The prank is the idea that a veil can stop a movement."

> "The prank played here was one of patience."

> "The prank here is the old regime fighting back against the inevitable; it is a comedy of errors where they pretend to have power while their walls crumble."

### Formula
```
The prank [is/here is/was] [the systemic irony] — [elaboration that connects ancient to modern].
```

## Writing Style

### Sentence Structure
- **Average sentence length:** 15.7 words
- Mix of short punchy observations and longer analytical sentences
- Heavy use of **em dashes (—)** in 57% of posts for parenthetical asides and dramatic pauses
- Minimal use of parentheses (4%) or quoted phrases

### Tone Spectrum
```
Academic ←————●—————→ Casual
         (leans intellectual but stays readable)
```

### Vocabulary Preferences
| Use This | Not This |
|----------|----------|
| "dissipate" | "destroy" |
| "mechanism" | "tool" |
| "posturing" | "pretending" |
| "apparatus" | "system" |
| "subjugate" | "oppress" |
| "monument" | "achievement" |
| "principate" | "leadership" |
| "farcical" | "ridiculous" |

### Rhetorical Devices
- **Historical parallels:** Almost every post connects modern events to Ancient Rome
- **Declarative authority:** States opinions as universal observations, not personal takes
- **Ironic distance:** Comments on absurdity from an elevated, detached vantage point
- **No first person:** Rarely uses "I" — prefers "we," "you," or third person
- **Rhetorical questions:** Used in 24% of posts, usually mid-caption

- Placed on the **final line**, separated from body by a blank line

## Caption Template

```
[Title in Title Case]

[Opening paragraph: 2-3 sentences setting up the modern observation.
Reference the video/image content directly. Use present tense for
commentary, past tense for historical reference.]

[Middle paragraph: 2-3 sentences drawing the Roman/ancient parallel.
Connect the modern scenario to a specific historical precedent.
Use specific Roman terminology — senate, republic, principate, etc.]

[Optional: Additional paragraph deepening the analysis or adding
a second historical parallel.]

[Closing paragraph with "The prank" motif: 1-3 sentences.
Reframe the entire topic as a systemic irony or cosmic joke.
This is the thesis — the big insight.]

#Hashtag1 #Hashtag2 #AncientRome
```

## What NOT to Do

- Don't use first person ("I think...")
- Don't use emoji in body text (only occasionally in titles, 4% of posts)
- Don't use bullet points or lists
- Don't ask for engagement ("Like if you agree!")
- Don't use more than 5 hashtags
- Don't break the intellectual register — no "lol," "bruh," or internet slang
- Don't write shorter than 1,000 characters (except for reactive hot takes)
- Don't forget the Roman parallel — it's the brand DNA
- Don't use ALL CAPS for titles or emphasis
